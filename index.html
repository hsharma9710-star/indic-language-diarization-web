Language diarization: English + up to two Indian languages

Upload a recording and get (a) an RTTM file, (b) the waveform coloured by language, and (c) one pooled audio file per language. Everything runs inside your browser: your audio is never uploaded or stored.

Name the languages if you know them (more accurate). Leave both on auto to detect them.

Research prototype, trained on synthetic code-switched speech: on real conversations about one 200 ms bin in four is wrong when the
  languages are named, and automatic language detection is less reliable (it often adds a similar-sounding second language).
  Known weak case: Hindi news with Indian-accented English can be labelled all Hindi; the command-line tool has an optional English check for this (see GitHub).
  First use downloads the 137 MB model (cached afterwards). Code, method and results:
  github.com/hsharma9710-star/indic-language-diarization.
